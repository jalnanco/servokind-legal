Data Deletion — Servokind tools

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Instagram

To remove the servokind-stats app's access, open Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites, and remove the app. Its tokens stop working immediately.

If you left a comment on a Servokind post and want the locally saved copy deleted, open an issue with your username. We delete it within 30 days and confirm on the issue.

TikTok

Revoke the app in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). The local tokens are then deleted.
